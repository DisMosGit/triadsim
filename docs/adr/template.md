# NNNN. Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made the decision

## Context

The forces at play: the problem being solved, the constraints (MVP scope, closed dependency
list, no external services, determinism in tests) and anything a reader needs to judge the
decision. Reference `docs/protocols/*.md`, `ROADMAP.md` or code instead of restating them.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice ("We use ...", "The store is addressed ..."), and the
rules that follow from it. State the interfaces, formats or boundaries that change.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Include the costs that are accepted deliberately:
extra code, lost generality, documentation that must be kept in sync.

## Alternatives

Each alternative that was seriously considered, and the concrete reason it lost.
